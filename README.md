# Shreyas Anand — Data, Design & Stories

My portfolio brings together data storytelling, visualization, AI, and cloud engineering.

**[Explore the portfolio](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/)**

## Selected work

- **[The Architecture of a Layover](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/final-project-part-three)** — A story about what average scheduled waits hide, using historical flight listings, long-wait comparisons, and local clock time. Follow the process in [Part I](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/final-project-part-one), [Part II](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/final-project-part-two), and [Part III](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/final-project-part-three).
- **[Data visualization examples](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/dataviz-examples)** — Four interactive Tableau views covering government debt, COVID-19 data, the financial crisis, and trust in news media.
- **[Critique by Design](https://anandshrey.github.io/Shreyas-Anand-portfolio/critique-by-design)** — A critique and redesign of a car-brand dependability ranking, with wireframes, peer feedback, and a final ranked chart and country map.

## About me

I'm pursuing a Master of Information Systems Management, specializing in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, at Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College. My interests span data visualization, AI and retrieval systems, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation.

This portfolio was developed for Carnegie Mellon's *Telling Stories with Data* course.
