02 / About me

An engineer's mindset.
A storyteller's curiosity.

sa.Shreyas Anand

I work at the intersection of data, AI, and cloud technology—with a growing focus on how those systems communicate with people.

I'm pursuing a Master of Information Systems Management, specializing in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, at Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College.

My experience spans research and industry: retrieval systems at the Defence Research & Development Organisation, Tableau dashboards at Sopra Steria, and cloud infrastructure and deployment automation at Quadrant Technologies.

Data & visualizationAI & retrieval systemsCloud & automation
What I set out to learn in this course +

My goals were to turn analysis into clear narratives, apply thoughtful visualization design, learn from user feedback, and explain the reasoning behind my design choices.

This portfolio brings those interests together through interactive charts, structured critique, and a research-led final story.
